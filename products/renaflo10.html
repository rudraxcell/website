Description

RENAFLO-10 (Dapagliflozin Tablets IP 10 mg) is a targeted, renoprotective sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitor indicated for the treatment of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD).

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) is a progressive, long-term condition in which the kidneys gradually lose their ability to filter waste and excess fluids effectively. CKD is frequently driven by conditions that place chronic hemodynamic and metabolic strain on the nephrons—most notably hypertension and glomerular disease, alongside metabolic disorders. In its early stages, most individuals with CKD experience few or no symptoms because the body can compensate for a substantial reduction in renal filtration. However, as glomerular injury progresses, kidney failure can develop, presenting with symptoms such as fatigue, weight loss, insomnia, peripheral edema, and shortness of breath.

Historically, management strategies were limited to controlling contributing conditions and downstream complications. RENAFLO-10 provides a dedicated disease-modifying therapy specifically proven to slow renal decline, preserve kidney function, and improve all-cause survival in patients with CKD—both with and without diabetes.

Composition

  • Each film-coated tablet contains: Dapagliflozin IP equivalent to Dapagliflozin 10 mg
  • Excipients: q.s.

Mechanism of Action

Dapagliflozin selectively inhibits sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) in the proximal renal tubules. As blood is filtered across the glomerulus, SGLT2 normally mediates the reabsorption of filtered sodium and glucose back into the circulation.

By blocking SGLT2, RENAFLO-10 increases the urinary excretion of sodium and glucose, which restores distal sodium delivery to the macula densa. This reactivates tubuloglomerular feedback, promoting afferent arteriolar vasoconstriction and directly reducing intraglomerular hypertension and hyperfiltration. Consequently, RENAFLO-10 reduces albuminuria, decreases tubular workload and hypoxic stress, and attenuates progressive nephron loss.

Renoprotective Clinical Benefits

In the landmark DAPA-CKD clinical trial evaluating adults with CKD (eGFR 25 to 75 mL/min/1.73m² and urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio 200 to 5000 mg/g), dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily demonstrated consistent benefits regardless of whether patients had underlying diabetes:

  • Slows Renal Function Decline: Significantly reduces the risk of a sustained ≥50% decline in estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR).
  • Delays Progression to Kidney Failure: Reduces the onset of End-Stage Kidney Disease (ESKD), defined as the need for maintenance dialysis, kidney transplantation, or sustained eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73m².
  • Reduces Proteinuria: Lowers intraglomerular pressure and urinary albumin excretion across diabetic and non-diabetic nephropathies.
  • Improves Cardiorenal Survival: Lowers the risk of hospitalization for heart failure, cardiovascular death, and all-cause mortality in CKD patients.

Indications

  • Treatment of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) in adults at risk of progression to reduce the risk of sustained eGFR decline, end-stage kidney disease, cardiovascular death, and hospitalization for heart failure.
  • Management of proteinuric / albuminuric chronic kidney disease (with or without type 2 diabetes).

Co-Administration in CKD Standard of Care

RENAFLO-10 is designed to complement standard-of-care renal and cardiovascular therapies. Clinical evidence from the DAPA-CKD trial demonstrates consistent efficacy and safety when dapagliflozin 10 mg is co-administered alongside maximally tolerated doses of renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS) inhibitors (ACE inhibitors or ARBs), as well as calcium channel blockers, beta-blockers, diuretics, antithrombotic agents, and lipid-lowering therapies, without increasing serious adverse events.

Dosage and Administration

The recommended dosage of RENAFLO-10 is one 10 mg tablet taken orally once daily, at any time of the day, with or without food, or as directed by a nephrologist or physician.

Warnings and Precautions

  • Volume Status: Assess volume status prior to initiating therapy and correct volume depletion if present, particularly in elderly patients or those on loop diuretics.
  • Initial eGFR Dip: A modest, reversible hemodynamic decrease in eGFR may be observed shortly after initiation due to reduced intraglomerular pressure; this reflects the renoprotective mechanism and is generally not an indication to discontinue therapy.
  • Genitourinary Infections: Monitor patients for signs and symptoms of urinary tract or genital mycotic infections and treat promptly if indicated.
  • Contraindications: History of serious hypersensitivity reaction to dapagliflozin or any excipient of the formulation. Not recommended for patients with polycystic kidney disease or those requiring immunosuppressive therapy for active lupus nephritis or ANCA-associated vasculitis.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place below 30°C. Protect from light and moisture. Keep out of the reach of children.

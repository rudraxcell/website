FERINEF

By RudraXcell Lifecare Pvt. Ltd.

Check availability here

Description

FERINEF Tablet is a dietary supplement containing iron that increases haemoglobin levels in the blood and supports red blood cell production. The tablet also supports oxygen transportation in the blood for metabolism and cell growth.

Composition

  • Elemental Iron

Key Benefits

  • Iron, as a mineral, serves as a carrier for oxygen and electrons
  • Acts as a catalyst in various biochemical reactions of the body
  • Plays a vital role in the transport and storage of oxygen in oxidative metabolism, cell growth and proliferation
  • Helps in metabolizing proteins and plays a role in the production of haemoglobin and red blood cells

Good to Know: This tablet can be beneficial for pregnant women with proper doctor consultation.

Effects of Deficiency

  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Shortness of breath
  • Headaches
  • Dizziness

Directions for Use

1 tablet daily or as directed by the physician.

Safety Information

  • Not for medicinal use
  • Do not exceed the recommended daily dosage
  • This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease
  • Consult a dietician before using this product if pregnant and lactating, nursing, taking any medication, or having any medical condition
  • Keep out of reach of children

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry, and dark place below 25°C
  • Protect from heat, light, and moisture
